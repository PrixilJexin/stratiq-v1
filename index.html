What we did

Five moves that changed the page

Heritage turned into a story, then into footfall at the anniversary sale.

01

Brand voice

“Crafted with tradition, chosen with love.” A voice that sounds like 21 years of handloom, not a discount banner.

02

Visual identity

A consistent gold-and-maroon palette across every post, so the grid reads as one brand.

03

Cinematic reels

Short-form video designed for discovery. The top reel reached 504K views, the second 102K.

04

Event moment

Everything pointed at one date: the anniversary sale on 10 and 11 July in Hosur.

05

Data-led tweaks

Weekly reads of what worked, then more of it. 12 of 18 posts crossed 1,000 views.

→

Result

Higher attendance and sales at the offline event, with Instagram as a direct source of new walk-in customers.
